More than a competition

MY GOSPEL TALENT (MGT) exists to help people discover, develop, and deploy their God-given talents for God's glory. The annual MGT event is one part of a larger platform — built for discovering talent, developing people, building careers, creating opportunities, and preserving gospel talent for the next generation.

The Annual MGT Event

MGT 2027

The annual MGT event is MGT's flagship stage — a live competition open to singers, rappers, spoken word artists, poets, dancers, instrumentalists, comedians, actors, and other creative performers. It's the most visible part of MGT, but it's built on the same mission that drives everything else: discover, develop, deploy.
